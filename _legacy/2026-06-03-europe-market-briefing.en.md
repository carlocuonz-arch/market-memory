## Market regime

Constructive but inflation-sensitive.

The shared-context regime remains:

- AI and quality growth continue to lead.
- Health remains a stabilizing defensive-growth allocation.
- Inflation, yields, energy prices and central-bank repricing remain the primary macro constraints.
- Crypto remains mostly a liquidity / Nasdaq-beta asset rather than a standalone defensive macro hedge.

## Top market-moving themes

### 1. Inflation and central-bank repricing

The main macro risk remains that inflation stays too persistent and forces central banks to stay restrictive for longer. This matters most for long-duration growth assets, including AI and technology.

Scenario map:

- Hotter inflation / hawkish central-bank tone: yields up, growth multiples down, banks relatively stronger.
- Cooler inflation / softer central-bank tone: yields down, tech and AI regain support, broader risk-on improves.

### 2. AI infrastructure leadership

The dominant equity narrative remains AI infrastructure rather than broad speculative growth.

Preferred exposures in the shared framework:

- Semiconductors
- Semiconductor equipment
- Datacenter infrastructure
- Electrification / power infrastructure
- High-quality profitable technology

### 3. Energy and geopolitical risk

Energy remains the key external macro risk for Europe because of its direct effect on inflation expectations, industrial margins and consumer purchasing power.

Scenario map:

- Energy price relief: inflation pressure eases, Europe cyclicals and growth benefit.
- Energy shock: inflation expectations rise, yields rise, Europe underperforms defensives.
